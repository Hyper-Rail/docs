Every trade on HyperRails is filled from **liquidity pools** that liquidity providers fund. If nobody is offering a pair, or the offers are too small, the trade can't be filled, no matter what the published rate says.

## Liquidity pools

A liquidity provider creates a pool for one currency pair and sets the rate they'll trade at.

| Pool type | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Funded** | Money is added to the pool right away. |
| **Unfunded** | The pool is approved by an admin first. |

Liquidity providers can edit, pause, resume, top up and delete their pools. A pool can't be deleted while a trade is in progress. Pools can also be given an expiry date. Expired pools are archived automatically and stay viewable, and pools with no balance are removed.

When a pair includes a stablecoin, the liquidity provider also picks the chain (network) for the pool.

## The Liquidity Marketplace

Pools are listed on the marketplace by currency pair. Each listing shows the provider's trading alias, never their business name.

| Listing field | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| `pair` | The currencies, as `A/B` |
| `rate` | The rate **this provider** offers. This is what a trade against the pool fills at |
| `availableBalance` | How much the pool can actually fill, in the first currency of the pair |
| `negotiationLimit` | `0` means the rate is take-it-or-leave-it |

Read listings with [Get Marketplace Listings](/api-reference/listings/liquidity-providers).

<Warning>
  `availableBalance` is the real constraint, and it's consumed as trades settle. Re-read listings right before you quote, and don't cache them. Asking for more than a pool holds fails with `Unable to fulfill requested volume at the moment.`
</Warning>

## How a trade is filled

When you request a quote, HyperRails **allocates** it across pools. The quote's `allocationResult` shows each allocation (pool, amount, rate) and the `weightedAverageRate` you get overall. `fullAllocation: true` means the whole amount was matched.

You can let HyperRails pick the pools, combine several pools in one order (liquidity aggregation), or name the pools yourself with a [multi-provider quote](/documentation/guides/request-for-quote).

## Errors that mean "no liquidity"

| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `No providers available for the given intent configuration` | Nobody trades this pair at all |
| `Unable to fulfill requested volume at the moment.` | Someone trades it, but not at this size |
