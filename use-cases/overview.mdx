## Which one fits?

| If you need to… | Start with |
|---|---|
| Hold treasury in dollars without a US bank account | [Stablecoin Payments](/use-cases/stablecoin-payments) |
| Let customers buy stablecoins with local currency | [On-ramp and Off-ramp](/use-cases/on-off-ramp) |
| Settle local stablecoin balances into a bank account | [On-ramp and Off-ramp](/use-cases/on-off-ramp) |
| Pay a supplier in another African currency | [Cross-border FX](/use-cases/cross-border-fx) |
| Give each customer an account number to pay into | [Collections and Payouts](/use-cases/collections-and-payouts) |
| Charge a customer's mobile money wallet for a trade | [Collections and Payouts](/use-cases/collections-and-payouts) |
| Earn on idle fiat or stablecoin balances by providing liquidity | [Supply Liquidity](/use-cases/liquidity-providers) |
