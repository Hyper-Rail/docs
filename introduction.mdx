Welcome to **HyperRails**, the platform for exchanging fiat and stablecoins using aggregated liquidity. HyperRails lets businesses on-ramp, off-ramp and exchange currencies across global payment corridors, with stablecoins as the settlement layer. Pricing, liquidity sourcing and settlement are handled for you, so you don't need to manage banks, blockchains or counterparties directly.

Our documentation is designed to help you understand our products, avoid common integration pitfalls, and get to market faster.

## What you can do with HyperRails

- **Convert fiat to stablecoins and back.** On-ramp local currency into USDT or USDC, and off-ramp stablecoins into local currency.
- **Exchange between fiat currencies.** Move between currencies like NGN, GHS, KES and USD, bridged through stablecoins.
- **Trade against aggregated liquidity.** Buy from a single pool or combine liquidity from several pools in one order.
- **Supply liquidity and earn.** As a liquidity provider, fund currency pairs, set your rates, and earn on executed trades.
- **Pay out globally.** Send fiat to bank accounts or stablecoins to wallet addresses.

## How HyperRails works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Liquidity is supplied">
    Liquidity providers fund currency pairs and set exchange pricing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rates are generated">
    HyperRails aggregates liquidity and displays the best available rates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trades are executed">
    Financial institutions and fintechs initiate exchanges and settle using available liquidity.
  </Step>
</Steps>
