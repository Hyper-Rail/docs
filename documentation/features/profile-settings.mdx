# Profile Settings

> Manage your personal login, password, and two-factor authentication.

Navigate to **Settings → General → Profile**.

## Personal Information
Edit your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and view your account **Email**
(click **Edit** to update; email changes require verification).

## Password
Click **Change password** to update your login password. You'll be asked to
confirm your current password first.

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
2FA adds a second verification step at login using an authenticator app
(e.g. Google Authenticator, Authy).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable 2FA">
    Toggle **Two Factor Authentication** to **On**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your code">
    Enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app in the **Enter code**
    field to confirm setup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate recovery codes">
    Click **Generate recovery codes**, then **Download .txt** and store the
    file somewhere safe. Recovery codes let you regain access if you lose
    your authenticator device — each code works once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Store your recovery codes offline. HyperRails cannot recover your account
  without them if you lose access to your authenticator app.
</Warning>
