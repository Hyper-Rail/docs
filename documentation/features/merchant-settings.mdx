# Merchant Settings

> Manage your business identity, branding, and support contact details.

Navigate to **Settings → General → Merchant settings**.

## Business Information

| Field | Editable? | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Trading name | ✅ | Display name shown across the dashboard. |
| Trading alias | 🔒 Contact admin | System-assigned short code for your business. |
| Legal business name | 🔒 Contact admin | Name on your incorporation documents. |
| Business registration number | 🔒 Contact admin | e.g. CAC/RC number. |
| Business industry | 🔒 Contact admin | Set during KYB verification. |
| Business website | 🔒 Contact admin | |
| Country / State / Province | 🔒 Contact admin | |
| Address | 🔒 Contact admin | |

Fields marked **Contact admin to make changes** are locked after KYB
verification for compliance reasons. Email
[support@hyperrails.io](mailto:support@hyperrails.io) to request a change.

## Business Logo
Click **Change logo** to upload your business logo (PNG/JPG, recommended
square aspect ratio). This appears on receipts and, where applicable,
customer-facing checkout flows.

## Contact Information
**Support Email** is the address HyperRails and your own sub-merchants use
to reach your team. Contact admin to change it.
