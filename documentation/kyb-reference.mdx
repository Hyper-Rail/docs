Every financial institution and fintech completes Know Your Business (KYB) verification before they can trade, on-ramp, off-ramp or move funds. This page lists what you'll be asked for. For the walkthrough, see [Onboard with us](/documentation/onboarding).

## What you'll provide

The onboarding flow is a checklist. You'll complete these sections:

| Section | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Service Type | How you'll use HyperRails: trading and moving money, supplying liquidity, or both |
| Business Information | Your registered business details |
| Business Operations | How your business operates |
| Contact Information | Who we should contact about your account |
| Business Owners and Directors | Details of all directors and owners, as required by local law |
| Documents | Supporting documents for the details above |

## Required Details and Documents

| Requirement | What to provide |
|---|---|
| Company Registration Number | Plus a supporting document, for example a Certificate of Incorporation |
| Tax ID | A valid tax ID, for example EIN, VAT or GSTIN, plus a supporting document |
| Directors and Owners | Information on every director and owner, as required by local law |
| Additional Documents | The onboarding flow shows required and additional document fields for your business |

<Tip>
  Make sure every document is clear, legible and in date. Unreadable or expired documents are the most common reason a submission is sent back.
</Tip>

## How checks work

- **Identity checks** run through Smile ID as part of onboarding.
- **AML checks** are carried out on all financial institutions and fintechs.
- **Ongoing monitoring.** After you're live, deposits and withdrawals are risk-checked before they go through, and flagged transactions are reviewed by our team.

## Timeline

Verification typically takes **1–3 business days**, excluding weekends and public holidays.

## When a submission needs fixing

If verification fails, the onboarding flow shows what needs fixing so you can correct it and resubmit. We'll also email you.

Common reasons:

- Missing or incomplete details, such as the business name or registration number
- Documents that don't match the information you entered
- Unreadable, illegible or expired documents
- Additional clarification requested by our compliance team

Our compliance team may ask for more documents from time to time. If they aren't provided, some transactions or payouts may be temporarily restricted, but your account won't be blocked entirely.
