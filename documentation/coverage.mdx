## Payment methods

| Method | Coverage | Learn more |
|---|---|---|
| Bank payouts with bank lookup and account name resolution | NGN, GHS | [Fiat payout process](/documentation/features/fiat-payout-process) |
| USD bank payouts | SWIFT/BIC. ACH and Fedwire aren't available yet | [Supported currencies](/documentation/supported-currencies) |
| Mobile money pay-in | Query live coverage with [List Mobile Money Currencies](/api-reference/hyperrail-quote/get-mmo-currencies). Providers in Ghana include MTN, Airtel and Telecel | [Let a Buyer Pay for a Trade](/documentation/guides/buyer-pays-for-a-trade) |
| Virtual accounts | By currency balance | [Give a Customer an Account](/documentation/guides/give-a-customer-an-account) |
| Stablecoin deposits and payouts | USDT and USDC on supported chains, from one multi-chain wallet. Payouts are gasless | [Stablecoin payments](/use-cases/stablecoin-payments) |

## Check coverage for your account

Coverage depends on the mode and on what's provisioned for your account. **Live mode supports fewer currencies than test mode.** Check before you build:

- `GET /balances/currency-list?mode=live`: the currencies your account can hold. See [Read Your Balances](/documentation/guides/read-your-balances).
- The **corridor checker** on the dashboard: whether a payment route or currency pair is supported.
- [Supported trade pairs](/api-reference/trade-pair/get-supported-trade-currency): the pairs available to trade.

Additional corridors and currencies are in controlled rollout. Contact [support@hyperrails.io](mailto:support@hyperrails.io) if you need one that isn't listed.
