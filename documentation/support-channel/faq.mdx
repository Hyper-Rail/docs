## Getting Started & Account Access

<Accordion title="Q1: What is HyperRails used for?">
A: HyperRails enables businesses to convert between fiat currencies and stablecoins, perform fiat-to-fiat exchange, and manage liquidity using stablecoins as the settlement rail.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q2: Is HyperRails an API or a dashboard application?">
A: HyperRails is both. You can perform on-ramp, off-ramp, FX, payout, and
  liquidity operations directly from the dashboard, **or** integrate
  programmatically using the [HyperRails API](/api-reference/introduction)
  once your account is approved for API access. Most merchants start on the
  dashboard and move to the API as their transaction volume grows.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q3: Who can use HyperRails?">
A: HyperRails is built for:
- Merchants performing on-ramp, off-ramp, and FX transactions  
- Exchange Clients supplying stablecoin or fiat liquidity  
- Enterprises managing cross-border treasury operations
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q4: How do I create a HyperRails account?">
A: Sign up directly on the HyperRails application and complete onboarding based on your role (Merchant or Exchange Client).
</Accordion>

## On-Ramp Transactions

<Accordion title="Q5: What is an on-ramp transaction?">
A: An on-ramp converts **fiat currency into stablecoins**, crediting the stablecoins to your HyperRails wallet.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q6: What fiat currencies and stablecoins are supported?">
A: HyperRails currently supports NGN, GHS, KES, ZAR, XOF, USD, GBP, and EUR for fiat currencies, major stablecoins like USDT and USDC with networks such as BASE, ETHEREUM, POLYGON and STELLAR.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q7: How long does an on-ramp transaction take?">
A: Settlement times depend on the fiat corridor, but most on-ramps complete within minutes to a few business hours.
</Accordion>

---

## Off-Ramp Transactions

<Accordion title="Q8: What is an off-ramp transaction?">
A: An off-ramp converts **stablecoins into fiat currency**, which is then paid out to a local bank account.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q9: Where can off-ramp funds be sent?">
A: Funds can be sent to supported local bank accounts linked to your HyperRails account.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q10: Are off-ramp rates fixed?">
A: Rates are executable at the time of transaction and reflect current liquidity and market conditions.
</Accordion>

## Fiat Currency Exchange

<Accordion title="Q11: How does fiat-to-fiat exchange work on HyperRails?">
A: Fiat-to-fiat exchange is executed via stablecoins:  
Fiat A → Stablecoin → Fiat B.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q12: Why does HyperRails use stablecoins for FX?">
A: Stablecoins enable faster settlement, lower costs, and better transparency compared to traditional correspondent banking.
</Accordion>

## Liquidity & Pricing

<Accordion title="Q13: Where does HyperRails’s liquidity come from?">
A: Liquidity is aggregated from multiple exchange clients across fiat corridors and stablecoin markets.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q14: Who can become a exchange client on HyperRails?">
A: Approved institutions and entities with fiat or stablecoin liquidity can onboard as Exchange Clients.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q15: How are exchange rates determined?">
A: Rates are calculated using aggregated liquidity and market pricing, with spreads dynamically adjusted based on transaction volume and liquidity depth.
</Accordion>

## Wallets & Treasury

<Accordion title="Q16: Does HyperRails provide stablecoin wallets?">
A: Yes. HyperRails provides merchant-owned stablecoin wallets with simplified key management.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q17: Can I hold both fiat and stablecoins on HyperRails?">
A: Yes. You can view and manage fiat balances, stablecoin balances, and in-flight transactions from a unified dashboard.
</Accordion>

## Security & Compliance

<Accordion title="Q18: Is HyperRails secure?">
A: HyperRails is built with enterprise-grade security, including wallet safeguards, role-based access, and transaction monitoring.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q19: Does HyperRails require KYC/KYB?">
A: Yes. Merchants and exchange clients must complete onboarding and verification before transacting.
</Accordion>

## Team & Access Control

<Accordion title="Q20: Can I add team members to my HyperRails account?">
A: Yes. You can invite team members and assign roles with specific permissions.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q21: Can different team members have different access levels?">
A: Yes. Roles allow you to control who can view balances, initiate transactions, or manage settings.
</Accordion>

## Support & Roadmap

<Accordion title="Q22: How do I get support if I encounter an issue?">
A: Support is available through the HyperRails dashboard and official support channels provided during onboarding.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q23: Will HyperRails support APIs in the future?">
A: Yes. The HyperRails API is live at `https://api.hyperrails.io/api/v1`
  and covers balances, rates, quotes, payouts, and dynamic (virtual)
  accounts. See the [API Reference](/api-reference/introduction) to get
  started. API access is granted per-partner during onboarding — contact
  [support@hyperrails.io](mailto:support@hyperrails.io) to enable it on your
  account.
</Accordion>
