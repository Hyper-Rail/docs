This guide is for teams already moving money with another provider who want to move some or all of that volume to HyperRails without disrupting customers.

## Migration overview

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get access">
    Onboard and complete KYB, then ask for API access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map your flows">
    Match each thing you do today to its HyperRails equivalent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build in test mode">
    Rebuild each flow against the test environment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run in parallel">
    Send a slice of live volume to HyperRails and compare.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cut over">
    Move traffic corridor by corridor, then retire the old integration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 1: Get access

1. [Onboard with us](/documentation/onboarding) and complete [KYB](/documentation/kyb-reference). Verification typically takes 1–3 business days.
2. API access is granted per partner during onboarding. Contact [support@hyperrails.io](mailto:support@hyperrails.io) to enable it.
3. Generate test keys from **Dashboard → Credentials → API Keys**. Each key is shown only once, then stored hashed, so save it straight away.

## Step 2: Map your flows

| What you do today | HyperRails equivalent |
|---|---|
| Get an indicative FX rate | [Get Rate](/api-reference/rate/get-rate), or [Rate Stream](/api-reference/rate/rate-stream) for live updates |
| Request a firm quote | [Intent](/documentation/guides/request-for-quote), [express quote](/documentation/guides/take-a-quote-in-one-call) or [multi-provider quote](/documentation/guides/request-for-quote) |
| Execute a conversion | Accept, authorize and complete the quote. See [Transaction lifecycle](/get-started/transaction-lifecycle) |
| Collect by bank transfer | [Virtual accounts](/documentation/guides/give-a-customer-an-account) |
| Collect by mobile money | [Accept a quote with a mobile money payment](/documentation/guides/buyer-pays-for-a-trade) |
| Hold stablecoins | [Stablecoin wallets](/documentation/guides/check-what-a-wallet-holds) |
| Pay out to a bank | [Fiat payout](/documentation/features/fiat-payout-process) |
| Send stablecoins | [Wallet payout](/api-reference/payout/wallet-payout) |
| Check balances | [Read Your Balances](/documentation/guides/read-your-balances) |
| Receive status updates | [Webhooks](/documentation/webhooks/overview) |

Things that commonly differ between providers, and how HyperRails handles them:

| Topic | On HyperRails |
|---|---|
| Credentials | One secret key works for every endpoint. See [Environments and credentials](/get-started/concepts/environments) |
| Duplicate protection | Your unique `reference` on payouts. Quote creation and virtual account creation aren't idempotent. See [Idempotency](/api-reference/idempotency) |
| Rates | The published rate is a reference. You trade at the quote's `weightedAverageRate`. See [Quotes](/get-started/concepts/quotes) |
| Liquidity | Trades are filled from marketplace pools. Check depth before quoting. See [Liquidity](/get-started/concepts/liquidity) |
| Webhook signatures | HMAC-SHA512, following the Standard Webhooks specification |
| Pagination | `page` and `size`, starting at page `0`. See [Pagination](/api-reference/pagination) |

## Step 3: Build in test mode

- Fund test balances and virtual accounts with the [Funding Simulator](/documentation/testing/funding-in-test-mode).
- Point a test webhook URL at your staging environment and check signatures with the [Webhook validator](/documentation/webhooks/validator).
- Rebuild your reconciliation around HyperRails references: your `reference` on payouts, `quoteId` on trades, and `x-hyperrails-webhook-id` on events.
- Work through the error cases on [Error codes](/api-reference/error-codes).

## Step 4: Run in parallel

- Generate live keys (shown only once, so store them in your secrets manager straight away) and configure your live webhook URL and secret. Live and test are configured separately.
- Route a small share of one corridor to HyperRails while your existing provider handles the rest.
- Compare rates, settlement times and success rates, and reconcile both sides daily.
- Check coverage for your account first. Live mode supports fewer currencies than test mode. See [Coverage](/documentation/coverage).

## Step 5: Cut over

- Increase HyperRails' share corridor by corridor once results match.
- Move any balances you hold with your old provider using your normal payout process with them.
- Keep your old integration available until in-flight transactions on it have settled, then retire it.
