description: "Get a deposit address for the right chain, share it safely, and confirm when funds arrive."

To receive USDT or USDC, give the sender your wallet's deposit address for the chain they're sending on. When the deposit lands, your wallet balance goes up and you receive a webhook.

## What you'll need

- Your **secret key**.
- Your `walletId`, or use `main`. See [Stablecoin wallets](/documentation/guides/stablecoin-wallets).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your deposit addresses">
    ```bash
    curl https://api.hyperrails.io/api/v1/wallets/WALLET_ID/deposit-addresses \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_SECRET_KEY"
    ```

    ```json
    {
      "walletId": "ac07cc78-7209-49ab-b3bd-28fa4b403c17",
      "addresses": [
        {
          "id": "227c4474-0e06-4862-906b-d9476c7dbe65",
          "chain": "base_sepolia",
          "address": "0xe5599e5092cbd398fd50b4a8f8ddba4909617b82",
          "explorerUrl": "https://sepolia.basescan.org/address/0xe5599e5092cbd398fd50b4a8f8ddba4909617b82",
          "status": "active",
          "createdAt": "2026-08-14T17:59:22.194204Z"
        }
      ]
    }
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick the right address">
    Choose the address where `chain` matches the sender's network **and** `status` is `active`. The list can include inactive addresses, so never just take the first one.

    For Stellar, also share `memoType` and `memoValue`. Several wallets can share one Stellar address, and a deposit without the memo can't be credited to you.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Share it with the sender">
    Tell the sender the exact chain, the address, and the memo if there is one.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm the deposit">
    Listen for the `wallet.credit_successful` [webhook](/documentation/webhooks/payloads). You can also read the wallet's activity:

    ```bash
    curl https://api.hyperrails.io/api/v1/wallets/WALLET_ID/activities \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_SECRET_KEY"
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Stablecoins sent on an unsupported network, to an inactive address, or to Stellar without the memo may be lost permanently.
</Warning>

<Note>
  In test mode, chain names are testnets, for example `base_sepolia`, `ethereum_sepolia`, `polygon_amoy`, `stellar_testnet` and `tron_nile`. Live mode returns mainnet names. Never reuse a test address in production.
</Note>

## When things go wrong

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Deposit never credited | Sent to an inactive address, or to Stellar without the memo | Only share `active` addresses; always include the Stellar memo |
| An address entry has no `address` field | The address is still being provisioned | Check the field exists before sharing it |
| **404** `wallet_not_found` | Wrong wallet ID, or a wallet from the other mode | List wallets with `GET /wallets` and check your key's mode |
